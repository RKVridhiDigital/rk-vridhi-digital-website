Questions & Answers

How the working process is decided

How do you decide which strategy a business needs?

Through business evaluation: market demand, customer behaviour, competition, current digital foundation, offer quality and growth stage. The recommendation follows the analysis, never the other way round.

Do you work with every business that enquires?

No. Every business is evaluated first. If a growth system is not the right fit at this stage, I say so and recommend the smaller, sensible step instead.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Strategy, consultation, execution and reporting are delivered remotely for clients across India and internationally.

Are marketing results guaranteed?

No responsible consultant can guarantee results. Performance depends on market demand, competition, customer behaviour, offer quality, budget, timing and execution. Every strategy is customised and continuously refined.
