Questions & Answers

Platform solutions explained

Which YouTube package is right for me?

Launch builds the foundation, Grow expands reach with keyword-led optimisation, and Authority is for brands building long-term positioning and organic search visibility.

Do I need YouTube Ads?

Not necessarily. These packages focus on organic YouTube channel management. Paid promotion is handled inside the ads packages on the Growth Packages page.

Are YouTube uploads included?

Upload management is included, but the client provides ready-to-upload videos. Video editing and paid YouTube promotion are not included.

Is LinkedIn Job Assistance a one-time payment?

Yes. Fresher, Professional and Executive are one-time profile optimisation services.

Can I upgrade from LinkedIn Starter later?

Yes. You can move from the Sales Funnel Starter to Ads Starter or the full LinkedIn Growth System at any point.

Which LinkedIn Business package should I choose?

Choose Sales Funnel Starter for organic authority, Ads Starter for paid B2B lead generation, and the Growth System when you want branding, content, ads and lead generation working together.

Can these services be combined with Complete Growth?

Yes. Platform solutions can be added to Complete Growth or AI-Powered Growth as part of one growth system.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Outcomes depend on content quality, market demand, competition, budget, timing and execution.
