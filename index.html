Background & Experience

I began in digital marketing by running campaigns, and quickly realised that campaigns alone rarely change a business. Since 2024 I have worked as an independent consultant with more than 200 businesses across 10+ categories, studying how markets, customers and offers actually behave.

That experience shaped a simple conviction: marketing decisions must begin with the business, not with the platform.

Consultant-First Mindset

I do not sell packages first. I evaluate the business, its market demand, competition and growth stage, and then recommend the smallest effective system that can move it forward.

AI is used where it genuinely improves speed, research quality, personalisation and automation — not as a marketing slogan.
