Questions & Answers

Choosing between packages

Which package is right for my business?

If you have an existing offer and only need reach, start with Ads Management. If you need visibility, trust and conversion built together, choose Complete Growth or AI-Powered Growth. A consultation confirms the right fit before you commit.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Most clients start smaller and upgrade once the market validates what is working.

Is Social Media Management available separately?

Social media is delivered inside Complete Growth and above, because it works with SEO, funnels and ads rather than in isolation. Standalone requirements can be discussed in a consultation.

Is SEO available separately?

Basic SEO is included in Complete Growth and Advanced SEO in AI-Powered Growth. Standalone SEO scopes are handled as a custom proposal.

What is the difference between Complete Growth and AI Growth?

Complete Growth builds the full presence and conversion system. AI-Powered Growth adds advanced SEO, AEO, GEO, advanced funnels, catalogue management and AI automation for efficiency and scale.

What are AEO and GEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) structures content so search engines and assistants can answer questions directly. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) structures entities and context so AI-powered search tools understand and cite the business correctly.

Does Full Ads Management include YouTube Marketing?

Yes, YouTube Marketing is included in Full Ads Management. Dedicated YouTube channel management is available on the YouTube and LinkedIn Business Solutions page.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Estimated outcomes indicate the intended direction of growth. Actual performance depends on market demand, competition, customer behaviour, budget, offer quality, timing and execution.
